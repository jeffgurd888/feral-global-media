# 💩 THE BULLSHIT FILTER — a Feral Global Media show

**Format:** a reaction livestream that pours claims through a five-sieve filter, live, and issues a **Trust Rating**. Chat brings the claims; the Filter does the pouring; the audience watches what clogs.

**Labels, honestly:** this is entertainment and opinion. Feral ratings are NOT lab verdicts — the lab's tiers live at Nexus Research and are never borrowed here. The Filter rates **claims and content**, never a person's character, and always shows its work: which sieve clogged, and why.

## The five sieves (the Feral tier filters)
Pour a claim through, top to bottom. Each sieve scores **Flows (2) · Drips (1) · Clogs (0)**.

1. **THE SCREEN — Source.** Who said it? Can you find them? Do they exist outside this clip/post?
2. **THE MESH — Evidence.** Shown, or just said? Is there data, a demo, a document — anything you can touch?
3. **THE TRAP — Incentive.** Who's selling what? Follow the money and the attention. A claim with a checkout link gets poured slower.
4. **THE RINSE — Reproduction.** Has anyone else checked it, repeated it, or poured it through their own filter?
5. **THE NOSE — Too-good test.** Does it promise the moon for nothing? The bigger the promise, the finer the mesh it must survive.

## Trust Ratings (0–10)
- 💎 **CLEAR WATER** (9–10) — passed everything. Rare. Say so when it happens.
- 🥤 **DRINKABLE** (7–8) — mostly solid; a little froth on top.
- 🌫️ **MURKY** (5–6) — mixed pour: some flows, some clogs. Handle with care.
- 🧦 **SOCK IN THE PIPE** (3–4) — mostly bullshit. Entertainment only; do not swallow.
- 💩 **FULL SEPTIC** (0–2) — pure bullshit. Flush it and wash your hands.

## Golden rules
1. **The Filter eats first.** Every stream opens by pouring one of the HOST'S OWN claims through — on the record, no mercy. (Episode 1 opener: Pelagic's own "10x energy surplus" and "skims microplastics from the gyres" — both clog at THE MESH: design targets, not demonstrated. That's the point: the Filter trusts no one, starting here.)
2. **Rate the claim, not the human.** Verdicts name the clog, never the soul.
3. **Receipts raise ratings.** Any claim can be re-poured in the Redemption Round — bring evidence, watch it climb. The Filter rewards proof, not pride.
4. **Ratings are never for sale.** A trust rating you can buy is itself FULL SEPTIC. (Tips and superchats buy the host coffee, never a score.)

## Segments
- **The Pour** — the claim goes through all five sieves, live.
- **The Clog Report** — the worst sieve gets its moment: what exactly got stuck, and what would unclog it.
- **Chat's Catch** — audience-submitted claims (headlines, ads, miracle cures, free-energy videos, guru math).
- **Redemption Round** — returning claims with receipts.
