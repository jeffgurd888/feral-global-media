# The Chain of Verification — working spec

**Feral Global Media · working spec, 2026-11-25**

## Purpose

Feral Global Media is a **human verified truth service**.

The problem it answers: news travels faster than fact, and almost nothing that reaches a reader carries any way to check who stands behind it. A claim arrives stripped of its road — no witness, no checker, no names — and the reader is asked to trust a brand, a feed, or an algorithm instead.

This algorithm works the other way. Every report carries its own road with it: a chain of named humans, each of whom checked the report and answered for the handoff. The reader doesn't trust Feral. The reader reads the chain and judges the humans in it.

The frame is **You, me and the world**: a witness at **ground zero**, a verifier who checks and answers, and the world that receives it — truth from anywhere on earth, in a fraction of a minute, carried by **6° of friendships and human trust**.

## The Tag

A **tag** is the atomic unit of the system. When a human verifies a report, they attach a tag to it. A tag records:

- **Who** — the person's name. Real names. There are no anonymous links.
- **When** — the time they checked it.
- **How they know the previous link** — their relationship to the person before them in the chain ("lives on my street," "my sister," "we work the same shift"). This is what makes the chain a chain of people rather than a chain of accounts.
- **What they checked** — in their own words: what they saw, confirmed, or verified, and how.

A tag is a person saying, in public: **"I answer for this handoff."**

## The Chain

A report is born at **ground zero** — the place the thing happened — filed by a witness who was there. That filing is the first tag, at degree 0°.

Every verification after that adds one tagged link, and the degree counts up: 1°, 2°, 3°, and onward as the report travels. The chain grows with the report and is published with it, inseparable from it. A report with no chain is not a Feral report — it is an untagged claim, and the system labels it as one (see Trust Ratings).

**The chain is the credential.** Not the outlet, not the follower count, not a blue check issued by a platform. The credential is the visible list of named humans who touched the report and what each of them did to it.

## The 3° Rule

Three degrees carry a report to the world:

- **You — 0°.** The witness at ground zero. You saw it, you file it, your name is on it.
- **Me — 1°.** The verifier. I check it — against what I can see, confirm, or know — and I tag it. My name goes next to yours, and I answer for the handoff.
- **The World — 3°.** Published, with the full chain attached, for anyone to read and judge.

Between Me and the World, more links may join — a second verifier, a carrier who passes it on intact — and each one tags. **6° is the reach:** chains extend outward through friendships, every link tagged, so a report from ground zero anywhere can reach everywhere through people who know each other. The rule for publication is a complete chain to a named witness: three degrees of named humans, minimum, between the event and the world.

## The Strength Rule

**A report is exactly as strong as its weakest tag.**

- If a link breaks — a verifier got it wrong, checked carelessly, or vouched for someone they don't actually know — the chain shows exactly where it broke, and the name on that tag carries it.
- There is no hiding a break and no quietly editing one away. A correction is a new tag, appended, signed, and dated — the chain keeps its whole history.
- **Reputation accrues to names.** A human whose tags hold up becomes a strong link: their tag carries weight because their record is visible. A burned name burns every chain it touches — which is why people guard their tags. The enforcement mechanism of the whole system is names and consequences, not a central office.

## Trust Ratings

The Bullshit Filter reads the chain and scores it. The rating describes the **chain**, not just the claim:

- **Clear Water 💎** — a complete chain: named witness at ground zero, verifications tagged at every degree, published at 3° with the whole road visible.
- **Drinkable 🥤** — chain holds and is checked more than once, still short of complete.
- **Murky 🌫️** — a named witness and one check; the road exists but is thin.
- **Sock in the Pipe 🧦** — a named witness only. A start — somebody real said it — but nobody has checked it yet.
- **Full Septic 💩** — no chain at all. An untagged claim. Flush it until somebody tags it.

Ratings are never for sale, and a rating can always be raised by strengthening the chain — more checks, better tags, receipts attached as new links.

## Roles

Three roles staff the network — **boots on the ground**, at every station from local to regional to national to global:

- **Reporter** — the witness. You are where it happens; you file from ground zero and put your name on it.
- **Verifier** — the checker. You confirm what a report claims — by seeing it, by knowing the reporter, by checking the record — and you tag it, answering for your link.
- **Carrier** — the passer. You move a verified report onward, intact, to the next degree — and your tag says you carried it unchanged.

One person can hold all three roles on different reports. Nobody holds any role anonymously.

## Abuse & Failure Modes

Named failure modes, stated plainly so they can be watched for:

- **The fake tag** — vouching for a check that never happened. Counter: tags are public and permanent; a fake tag is a signed lie attached to a name forever.
- **The rubber stamp** — a verifier who tags everything and checks nothing. Counter: the strength rule — their chains keep breaking at their link, in public, and their name's weight falls.
- **The collusion ring** — a closed circle tagging each other's reports to fake a strong chain. Counter: tags record *how each link knows the previous one*; a chain whose every relationship is the same small circle reads as exactly that, and outside links can be demanded before a rating rises.
- **The broken telephone** — a carrier quietly altering a report in transit. Counter: a carrier's tag attests the report was carried intact; the original witness's tag and wording stay at the head of the chain for anyone to compare.
- **The pile-on** — many weak tags dressed up as strength. Counter: the rating weighs the weakest link and the quality of checks, not the count of tags.

The system's honest limit: it cannot make a liar honest. It can make lying expensive, visible, and permanently attached to a name — and make honesty cheap, fast, and credited.

## What It Is Not

- **Not an algorithm that decides truth.** No software scores the facts. Humans check; the chain records; readers judge.
- **Not a platform's verification badge.** No central authority issues trust. Trust is the chain, or there isn't any.
- **Not anonymous.** Anonymity is the thing this system exists to replace.
- **Not a newsroom.** There is no desk deciding what the world may see. There are stations — local, regional, national, global — staffed by people who tag what they can answer for.
- **Not the lab.** This is a Feral system, on Feral's own ground, under Feral's own name. It borrows nothing from any other house and answers to its own record.
